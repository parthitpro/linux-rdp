Gujarat is located on the western coast of India along the Arabian Sea.

The state capital is Gandhinagar while Ahmedabad serves as its largest commercial hub.

Gujarati is the official language spoken widely across all regions.

Indus Valley Civilisation settlements such as Lothal and Dholavira highlight ancient port engineering.

Lothal contains one of the world earliest known tidal docks for maritime trade.

Dholavira is an archaeological treasure exhibiting advanced water harvesting reservoirs.

Gola Dhoro is another ancient Harappan site known for craftsmanship in copper and shell seals.

Coastal cities including Bharuch and Khambhat functioned as prominent historical sea trade centers.

The state shares its northern land border with Rajasthan and eastern border with Madhya Pradesh.

Maharashtra borders Gujarat to the south while the Arabian Sea lies to the west.

The Rann of Kutch represents a vast seasonal salt marsh expanding across the northern district.

The Gir National Park provides a sanctuary for the only wild Asiatic lion population worldwide.

Marine National Park in the Gulf of Kutch protects coral reefs and diverse marine biodiversity.

Sabarmati Ashram in Ahmedabad played a vital historic role in India freedom movement led by Mahatma Gandhi.

Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel known as the Iron Man of India originated from this western state.

The Statue of Unity situated on the Narmada River is recognized as the world tallest statue.

Major rivers flowing through the plains include the Narmada Tapi Mahi and Sabarmati.

The state features an extensive coastline stretching over sixteen hundred kilometers.

Traditional folk dances including Garba and Dandiya Raas are performed vibrantly during Navratri.

Gujarat is a leading global producer and exporter of textiles diamonds and petrochemicals.

Diamond cutting and polishing industries are heavily concentrated in the city of Surat.

Petrochemical refining facilities in Jamnagar operate among the largest manufacturing complexes globally.

The state operates major commercial cargo ports such as Kandla and Mundra handling extensive shipping volume.

Dhokla Thepla Khandvi and Undhiyu constitute classic culinary specialties of regional cuisine.

Gujarat continues to maintain robust infrastructure development renewable solar parks and industrial growth.
